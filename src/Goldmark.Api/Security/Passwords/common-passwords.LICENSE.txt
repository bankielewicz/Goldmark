common-passwords.txt: passwords Goldmark refuses because they are common (DEC-038, DEC-040)

Derived from the top-1M list of Pwdb-Public by Ata Hakçıl:
  https://github.com/ignis-sec/Pwdb-Public, commit 9b53917431ed5cd57cd6cbec8c3a19cf9a5cd8dd,
  wordlists/ignis-1M.txt, SHA-256 e9a88f67aafe65496682dc374559ee714e978bee50314767494c3e37a18c9fc8.
Changes: only the entries of 12 to 128 characters are kept, lower-cased, de-duplicated and sorted;
lines that are not valid UTF-8 or contain control characters are dropped. Rebuild with
tools/common-passwords/build-common-passwords.mjs.

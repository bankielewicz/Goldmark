“Show Goldmark as of”

Fluent v9 Dialog (modal, aria-label “Choose a point in time”), Field + DatePicker and TimePicker (the compat packages the audit log already uses), Buttons. Errors show under the fields on Apply; focus returns to the first field in error.

Light · empty, as it first opens (focus in Date)
Light · a moment typed, with the local hint
Light · reopened with a moment chosen: “Return to now”
Light · error: nothing entered
Light · error: the future
Light · error: the year (the audit log’s wording)
Dark · a moment typed
Dark · reopened with a moment chosen
Dark · error: the future
Sample data (fictional). The dialog is 480 px wide in the app (drawn at 410 here to sit three across); on a phone it fills the width less 16 px each side. “Yesterday” and “7 days ago” keep the current time of day; a quick choice fills the fields and leaves Apply to the person.

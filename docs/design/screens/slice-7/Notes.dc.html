Goldmark · slice 7 · DEC-047 · US-42, US-43, FR-CM-5, FR-IM-4

Notes: components, details to approve, design system changes

Slices 3 to 6 are unchanged when no moment is chosen. The Search, CI and Graph pages here are the approved components with an as-of state added; the History tab and the As-of dialog are new.

Details these boards settle that the owner hasn’t decided

Each is drawn as the default. Approving the boards approves these; say which to change.

  1. 1. One As-of control, in the top bar.It works on Search, a CI’s page and the Graph explorer.Why: the design system already reserves the slot; the moment is one thing that travels between three screens, so one control in one place says so. A control per screen (HANDOFF row 7) would cost: three toolbars to change (Search’s and the Graph’s options bar are already full at 1024, so it would land in the Filters drawer there), three places for the on-state, and a person moving from Search to a CI would have to re-find it. HANDOFF row 7 needs its wording updated if this is approved.
  2. 2. On other screens the control is disabled and the moment is kept.It reads “As of: Now” there (the page shows the present) with the Tooltip “This page always shows the present.” Going back to Search, a CI or the Graph by the navigation restores the moment.Why: a person checking the audit log mid-investigation shouldn’t lose their place. Against clearing on leave: simpler to build, but a detour silently drops the past view. The moment is kept for the browser session only; a link without asOf opens the present.
  3. 3. History is a timeline with a detail pane at 1100 and up.Below that, one column where an entry expands in place, one open at a time.Why: the diff table needs width, and a pane lets Dana arrow down the list and watch the detail change. Against expanding in place everywhere: one layout to build, but the list jumps as entries open and a four-column diff is cramped inside a 40% column.
  4. 4. “Created” lists every starting value, each as Added.Why: for Ingrid the first version is evidence like any other, and it is the only place a value that never changed appears in the history. Showing only “Created” would be less to read but would send her to “View this CI as of this time” for the same facts.
  5. 5. No filter or search inside a CI’s history.Why: most CIs have tens of changes, day headings and “Show 50 more” are enough, and the filter by source arrives with sources in slices 10 and 11. A busy host (132 changes on esx-mx-014) is where this will be felt first.
  6. 6. Quick choices: 1 hour ago, Yesterday, 7 days ago.Each fills the fields; the person still presses Apply. “Yesterday” and “7 days ago” keep the current time of day.Why: they cover “what just changed”, “before last night’s window” and “last week” without typing. Filling rather than applying lets the person adjust the time first.
  7. 7. Minutes, not seconds, in the picker.The picker’s moment is the start of that minute. “View this CI as of this time” on a history entry uses the entry’s exact moment, so the address can carry seconds while the top bar and banner show minutes.Why: nobody plans to the second, but two changes in the same minute (the two at 09:12 here) must still be separable from the history.
  8. 8. An ending nobody is on record for.Who reads “Unknown” (subtle, no avatar), and the detail carries the note “The audit entry that recorded who ended it is no longer kept.”Why: it states the cause (retention) and doesn’t suggest a fault or a hidden actor.
  9. 9. A subtle badge “Ended since” on a relationship in a past view.Not in the handoff. Why: it is the relationships’ twin of “Deleted since” on an attribute, and stops someone planning against a dependency that is gone. Drop it if a past view should show nothing about the present.
  10. 10. Disabled actions stay visible and focusable.Edit, Retire, Restore, New CI, Add relationship and each row’s Edit and Delete keep their place, greyed, aria-disabled, with the one Tooltip on hover and on focus. A role that never had the action still doesn’t see it.Why: the layout doesn’t jump between now and then, and keyboard users can reach the explanation.
  11. 11. The banner’s icon is the clock-arrow, not the warning triangle.Why: it matches the control that put the person there, and nothing is wrong. Intent stays warning (amber), as the handoff says.
  12. 12. Small wording choices.The short label keeps the year when it isn’t this year (“01 Mar 2019 09:00”). “Nothing existed yet” adds “Nothing had been entered in Goldmark yet.” The “didn’t exist” page adds when the CI was created. A history entry’s relationship keeps the handoff’s present-tense Direction (“This CI depends on esx-mx-011”) under an “Ended” badge.

Three things in the handoff to check

  • The local-time hint for the history heading. The handoff gives “16:12 your local time” for 09:12 UTC, but “16:00 your local time” for 14:00 UTC. Those are two different offsets. The boards use UTC+2 throughout, so the heading reads “11:12 your local time”.
  • The sample CI’s data. Slice 6 drew app-erp-db01 with Cost centre CC-4410 and runs_on esx-mx-014 since 2026-10-02. This slice’s history says Cost centre was deleted on 2026-10-09 and the move to esx-mx-014 was on 2026-10-15, so the CI page here follows the history. Slice 6’s boards aren’t touched.
  • Quotation marks in the count line. The approved Search page writes ‘erp’; this handoff writes “erp”. The as-of count line follows this handoff; the present-time line is left as approved, so the two differ until one is chosen.

Changes to the design system this slice needed

  • States board, “As of”: the design system draws it as an info MessageBar with a 24 px button and no local time. The handoff asks for a warning MessageBar, so the boards use the warning tokens the Retired state already has, a 28 px “Return to now”, the clock-arrow icon and the local-time hint. The States board should be redrawn to match.
  • Shell board, the top-bar slot: filled, after the search box and before the theme toggle. The Shell board should replace its empty slot with the three states on the top-bar board here.
  • Badge vocabulary: four subtle (tint-subtle) badges join it: “Deleted since”, “Ended since”, “Shown above”, and the diff’s “Added / Removed / Changed” (one style, the word carries the meaning).
  • Icon set: one icon per kind of change (created, changed, removed, retired, restored, relationship added, changed, ended) and the clock-arrow. All bundled Fluent icons: AddCircle, Edit, SubtractCircle, Archive, ArrowCounterclockwise, Link, ArrowSwap, LinkDismiss, History.
  • No new tokens, colours or components. Disabled buttons use Fluent’s disabled neutrals.

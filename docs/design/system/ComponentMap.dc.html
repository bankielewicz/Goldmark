Deviations from Fluent defaults

  1. Brand ramp (BrandVariants 10–160, muted gold). The only colour input; Fluent's own mapping from ramp to tokens is unchanged.
  2. Degraded uses Fluent's "severe" colours (dark orange), not "warning" (yellow): yellow sits too close to the gold brand. See the badge board.
  3. Density: a Compact preference switches Fluent's size prop to "small" on tables and controls; no custom sizes.
  4. No other overrides: no custom greys, radii, shadows, type sizes or focus styles.

Changes to the mockups (this system wins)

  1. Dark primary buttons are deep gold (shade 70) with white text, Fluent's mapping, not light gold (#e6b450) with dark text.
  2. Dark brand text is shade 100 (#c28a2a, 4.8:1), not #e6b450.
  3. Status colours are Fluent's palette tokens, not the mockups' --dn / --wn / --ok values; Degraded becomes orange.
  4. Neutrals are Fluent's; the mockups' greys (e.g. --bd #e0e0e0, --fg2 #5c5c5c) move to the nearest Fluent token.
  5. Focus is Fluent's black/white two-tone ring everywhere, never a gold ring.
  6. The As-of control is an empty slot until slice 7 (screens 4, 5, 6).
  7. Nav hides items for screens not yet built; Settings and Apps & integrations stay tagged P1.
  8. The TOTP QR code (screen 2) is an image Goldmark serves, not inline data.

00-SharedStates and every screen mockup carry the old colour values; they are redrawn in their slice's screen handoff. The States board here supersedes 00-SharedStates.
